Help

Quick guide to using PharmaTrack

🚀 Getting Started

Dashboard gives you a live overview of today's sales, low-stock items and an auto-generated summary of your pharmacy's performance.

Inventory is where you add, edit and track medicines, quantities and prices. Sales / POS is where you record a sale, and Sales Log / Invoices let you search and reprint past transactions.

📦 Managing Inventory

Go to Inventory → + Add Medicine to create a new item, or click Edit on any row to update its price, quantity or picture. The Reorder Level field controls when an item is flagged as "Low Stock" on the Dashboard.

🛒 Making a Sale

On Sales / POS, pick a medicine and quantity, click Add to Cart, then repeat for each item. When ready, choose whether to auto-print a receipt and pick a paper size (80mm / 58mm thermal or A5 / A4), then click + Add to log to complete the sale and update stock automatically.

💳 Expenses

Go to Expenses → + Add Expense, fill in the description, category and amount. You can also generate a printed expense receipt at the same time using the same checkbox and paper-size option used for sales.

💾 Backup & Excel

In Settings, use Export Backup regularly to save a full copy of your data as a .json file — keep it somewhere safe. Use Import Backup to restore it later, for example on a new device.

Use Export to Excel to get a spreadsheet of your products, sales, expenses and customers for reporting outside the app. Import from Excel lets you bulk-update your product list and customers from a spreadsheet.

❓ Troubleshooting

All data is stored locally in this browser. Clearing your browser data will erase it unless you've exported a backup first. If something looks wrong, try exporting a backup before using Reset demo data in Settings.
